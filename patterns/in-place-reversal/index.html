In-place list reversal

Reverse all or part of a linked list by rewiring next pointers as you walk, using three pointers and no extra memory.

Coding pattern

Picture it. Turning a conga line around. Each person lets go of the shoulder in front and grabs the person behind instead. You only need to remember who was behind you, who you are, and who was in front before you let go.

Recognise it

  • The problem asks to reverse a linked list, a sub-range of it, or every group of k nodes.
  • You need to compare a list with its reverse (palindrome list) in O(1) space.
  • Reordering a list by interleaving its halves.

The template

prev = null, curr = head
while curr:
    next = curr.next        ← save the rest
    curr.next = prev        ← flip one pointer
    prev = curr; curr = next
prev is the new head

How it works

Reversing a whole list flips one pointer per node. Reversing a sub-range needs a little bookkeeping: walk to the node just before the range (a dummy head makes this work even when the range starts at the first node), reverse the range, then reconnect both ends.

reverse positions 2..4 of 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5
before  = node 1, first = node 2
reverse 2 → 3 → 4 into 4 → 3 → 2
before.next = node 4; first.next = node 5
result: 1 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 5

k-group reversal repeats that for every full block of k nodes and leaves a final short block as it is.

Cost of each operation

OperationTimeWhy
Reverse a whole listO(n) time, O(1) spaceOne pointer flip per node.
Reverse a sub-rangeO(n)Walk to the range, reverse it, reconnect.
Reverse in groups of kO(n)Each node is visited a constant number of times.
Palindrome listO(n) time, O(1) spaceFind the middle, reverse the second half, compare.

Common mistakes

  • Overwriting curr.next before saving it, losing the rest of the list.
  • Forgetting to reconnect the reversed piece to the nodes before and after it.
  • Not using a dummy head, so reversing a range starting at position 1 needs a special case.
  • Reversing a short final group in k-group reversal when the problem says to leave it.
